About Us

NCESS Cosmetics is dedicated to creating premium skincare and beauty products tailored for melanin-rich skin tones.

We blend science, culture, and luxury to create beauty solutions that feel elegant, effective, and deeply personal.

Founder: Princess Ruth Adjei is the visionary behind NCESS Cosmetics, bringing passion, purpose, and elegance to every part of the brand.

Founded in Accra, Ghana, NCESS Cosmetics was created to redefine beauty for people who want products that are both luxurious and deeply respectful of their skin, heritage, and identity. We believe skincare and cosmetics should feel indulgent, effective, and empowering, whether you are building a daily routine or preparing for a special occasion.

Our brand is rooted in quality, innovation, and the celebration of natural beauty. Every product is crafted with care to bring out confidence, radiance, and a healthy glow while honoring the richness of melanin and the elegance of African beauty traditions.

At NCESS, we are more than a beauty brand; we are a movement that inspires self-love, cultural pride, and modern luxury through every experience we create.
